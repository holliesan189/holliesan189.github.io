Once Upon a Contract

You enter the lodge to find the prince sitting by the fireplace, plucking away at an instrument that sounds better in theory than in practice. The door opening makes him drop it.

He looks up startled.

"Who are you?"

You produce the royal contract and hold it out for him to see.

He studies the paper, then you, then the paper again.

"Oh."

A pause.

"You're looking for me?"

You explain the situation, the search, the trouble you've gone through to find him. He listens, looking almost apologetic. The prince explains he didn't realize he was gone for so long.

"Well I suppose I should thank you. Though I wonder...."

Uh Oh

"I could use someone with your talents. Someone who knows how to keep a secret, find their way through trouble, and perhaps keep me alive. Forget bringing me home. Come with me instead!"

A Change of Terms

You could accept the prince's offer. He's a terrible musician but a rather likable companion. He knows stories of forgotten kingdoms, old legends, and places most people wouldn't dare venture. He might even know where a few trasures are buried.

You could carry on as you were. There are benefits to working alone after all. You answer to no one, spilt no rewards, and get to decide which trouble is worth getting into. You could take on another contract, perhaps even one from H. There are plenty of people willing to pay for your patricular talents.

You might expect H to have something to say about all this. She does so rather enjoy having the final word. But alas, no message, no intructions, not even a "perhaps". Seems you're on your own this time. What will you do?
