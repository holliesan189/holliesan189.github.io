A Cloak Fit for a King

Finding a needle in a haystack would be easier than finding an honest face at the Fool's Gold Tavern.

You lean against the wall, the best seat in the house for a bounty hunter.

The dress code is simple, dont be noticed. Cloaks are pratically mandatory here. What's not in style is nursing your mead. Yet this particular patron has a full glass. It's hard not to notice.

You look closer at them and realize that's not the only thing out of place. The cloak itself is dusty and worn, but the clasp holding it together is polished silver.

You glance up, only to find the patron watching you in return.

Time to look occupied. You signal the barkeep for another drink, settling in as though you have nowhere else to be. It's a higher tab certaintly, but think of the trail they'll leave when they think no one's watching.

Before you can pay, the patron rises and slips out the door.

How much of a head start should we give them?

You may give them thirty seconds of a full two minutes. Choose your time wisely. H does so dislike waiting, and the Crown even less so.
