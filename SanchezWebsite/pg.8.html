Lodge and Found

Your persuasion pays off. You reassure him that your contract is not with him. The royal family memeber relaxes, and you leave the conversation without so much as scuffing your boot.

With a drink promised and a little trust earned, you ask where else a member of the court might go to unwind.

The royal memember offers his best suggestion with the understanding that you forget his own tavern adventure. "If I wanted privacy I would head north of here. There's an old hunting lodge that the royal family hardly uses anymore. It's far enough from prying eyes but close enough to home."

This has to be it!

You thank him for his help and make your way to the lodge. For a prince who's managed to disappear, Feran seems to have left an awful lot of breadcrumbs.
