A Royal Pain in the Woods

You waited the perfect amount of time.Not too eager, not too late. The trail is still warm and you follow it deeper into the forest. A flash of silver catches your eye between the trees.

You know better than to leap at that fool's gold. You wait until the figure stops, and the silver catches light once more.

A member of the royal family sure, a prince? No.

He takes a nervous step back

"You can't take me back to the castle"

This is a delicate situation. He isn't the prince you're looking for, but hes still a member of the royal family. You can't simply leave him in the woods, especially when he might know something about Prince Feran.

Besides, it took you long enough to find one runaway royal. Who knows how much trouble finding another would be?

How Do You Calm Him Down?

He's clearly frightened and convinced you're here to drag him back to the castle. You may try to talk him down or attempt to subdue him. Either way, you'll have to act carefully. H does so dislike poor judgement. The trouble is, it rarely annouces itself beforehand.
